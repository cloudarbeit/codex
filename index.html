DESIGNED TO BE AUDITABLE

Useful because it shows its work.

This project turns Codex-assisted product development into a small tool with explicit evidence boundaries—not an autonomous sales agent.

A

Evidence remains attached

Every observation has a source title, HTTPS URL, user-selected classification, and confidence label. The app never fetches the page or claims to verify your note.

B

Unknown means unknown

Signals and risks are classifications you choose. Open questions get their own list. Coverage counts do not turn into a fabricated lead score.

C

Approval has no send button

The report has a review marker and an editable outreach draft. There is no email connection, contact action, or hidden automation.

D

Local-first, portable data

There is no server database or analytics in this demo. Saving is opt-in and stays in your browser storage; JSON and Markdown exports give you control of your files.

Implementation notes and verification boundaries

This standalone tool runs deterministically in the browser. It does not call an AI model, read a website, send data to an API, or send outreach. It creates a source-backed research pack and editable internal brief that a person can inspect and export.
